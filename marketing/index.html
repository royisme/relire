Read, tap, say it back, review.

Everything starts from the text in front of you: the word you tap, the sentence you play back and the cards you review all come from what you are reading.

01 · READ

Start reading

03 · SAY IT

Say it out loud

Select a sentence to listen or record yourself; the last three takes of each sentence stay on your device. Recording needs no key. Graded readers use Device voice; select it in Settings for keyless playback elsewhere.

Keep what you read

Save words and review them with FSRS-6 spaced repetition; reviewed conjugation for 19 verbs and noun-gender endings to practise.

Questions

Before you start.

A browser, the French you want to read, and, for the AI features, your own key.

Is Relire free?

Yes. Relire is open source under the MIT License. AI features use your own key for the provider you choose (Gemini, an OpenAI-compatible service or Claude); local Ollama needs none.

Do I need an account?

No. Relire has no account and no app server of its own; articles, words, recordings and settings stay in your browser. The only backend is an optional read-only content service, which never receives your AI key, answers, articles or recordings.

What works without a key?

The three graded readers, the noun-gender reference and the conjugation of the 19 reviewed verbs. The sound chart and sound pairs play through the speech provider you selected; choose Device voice in Settings to hear them without a key.

Who reviewed the content?

The maintainer reviewed the 19 conjugation verbs and the noun-gender pack (17 ending tendencies, 337 example nouns). The graded readers were written and reviewed by AI models — a different model reviewed each text than the one that wrote it — not by a teacher, and each reader says so on its page.

Is this an official TCF Canada preparation tool?

No. Relire is an independent practice tool, not a mock-exam platform, and is not affiliated with France Éducation international or IRCC. AI pronunciation scores are estimates, not official exam marks.

Can I inspect the source or report a problem?

Yes. The complete project is on GitHub, including its issue tracker.
