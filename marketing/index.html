Questions

Before you start.

Relire is intentionally lightweight: a browser, your own AI key for AI-powered features, and the French text you want to learn from.

Is Relire free?

Yes. The project is open source under the MIT License. AI features use your own Gemini API key, so any provider usage is tied to your own account and quota.

Do I need an account?

No Relire account is required. Your articles, vocabulary and progress are stored in your browser.

What works without an API key?

You can open the app, read sample or imported texts and use locally stored study data. AI explanations, generated speech and pronunciation assessment require a configured provider key.

Is this an official TCF Canada preparation tool?

No. Relire is an independent learning tool focused on reading and pronunciation practice. Pair it with official exam information, mock tests and teaching when preparing for TCF Canada.

Can I inspect the source or report a problem?

Yes. The complete project is on GitHub, including its architecture and issue tracker.
